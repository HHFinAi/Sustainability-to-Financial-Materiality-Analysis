# Research update: 16 October 2026

## What changed since the October 8 release

The [existing Microsoft cooling case](../microsoft-ai/README.md) quantified water/energy trade-offs, protected workload and a separate capacity project. Its original assumptions and outputs remain preserved. This extension adds a primary-source project register and a separate common-horizon model; it does not silently replace the earlier counterfactual.

| Previous boundary | New work | Remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Generic design/basin context | Dated Mount Pleasant, Caledonia and Georgia records; actual policy texts | No site permit, commercial tariff, phase MW or project cash flow verified |
| Delay moved operations, with holding costs outside scope | Explicit month-zero commitment, hardware at commissioning and monthly holding cost | Real spending schedules and penalties unknown |
| One hardware replacement schedule | Separate replacement/tax lives, cash replacements and residual-value challenge | Economic life and market resale uncalibrated |
| Water-linked disruption | Conditional operational incident and NIST OT diligence handoff | No issuer failure frequency or control-effect estimate |
| No group bridge | Dated FY2026 group denominators and hypothetical per-share scale | No calibrated common-equity valuation or market mispricing |
| Source-quality examples | EIA data re-extraction, queue-population and policy-scope checks | No new measured LLM performance or human-review result |

**Investment judgment added:** value the delivery window and contract allocation before assuming a power workaround earns an adequate return. The illustrative gas route's narrow positive reference value disappears with a six-month commissioning delay. An actual route decision still requires evidence that the alternative can be delivered on the assumed terms.

**Source discipline added:** annual power-price comparisons use one EIA vintage and complete calendar years; raw source cells and bytes are recorded. Generator queues are kept separate from data-center load connections. The unavailable LBNL workbook is an explicit access limit, not a completed reconstruction.

**Editorial correction:** removed the duplicated MS4-MS6 and extension-review block from the earlier Microsoft source register. Source content and historical baseline were otherwise retained.

This is a new analysis prepared 16 October 2026, not a prediction published before the observed project events. Changes in the case are attributable to the newly reviewed sources and explicit modeling choices; no realized investment performance is claimed.
