## Updating the evidence

1. Add the new primary source with publisher, observation period, date, locator and limitations.
2. Match its parcel, phase and legal entity before changing status or capacity.
3. Preserve the previous dated record in version history; an estimate becoming stale is not an observed adverse outcome.
4. Change a model input only after establishing the cash-flow mechanism and responsible counterparty. Keep alternatives as labelled assumptions until verified.

Suggested financial links are **METHOD/INFERENCE**, not source facts. Any investment conclusion remains conditional on contract terms, prices, costs and utilization. The separate [issuer context](issuer_context.json) supports scale comparisons only.
